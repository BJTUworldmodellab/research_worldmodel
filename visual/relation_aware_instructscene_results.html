Pipeline

1
Generate with InstructScene

Use the official pretrained scene-graph and layout checkpoints.

2
Parse explicit relations

Extract phrases such as left of, right of, behind, in front of, and close right.

3
Verify realized layout

Check whether generated boxes satisfy the selected spatial relation triples.

4
Repair translations

Apply two bounded passes with close distance 0.75 and far distance 1.6.

Takeaway

Current conclusion: this is now a stronger paper seed for explicit spatial-relation realization. The relation metric is convincing across bedroom, living-room, and dining-room splits, and the box-level geometry checks do not show overlap or out-of-bound degradation. A submission-ready package still needs full mesh collision, diversity, rendered qualitative examples, and shared-protocol comparisons before making stronger SOTA claims.

Best storyline: InstructScene can know the relation at the graph level but fail to realize it in geometry. Relation-Aware InstructScene exposes and repairs this graph-to-layout grounding gap.
